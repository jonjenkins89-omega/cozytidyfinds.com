About Us

Hey, we're Cozy Tidy Finds

This whole thing started because my partner and I kept getting burned by Amazon purchases. You know the cycle: scroll through gorgeous product photos, read a few reviews, click "Add to Cart," and then three days later you're holding something that looks nothing like the listing and falls apart before the weekend. We got tired of it.

So we started keeping a running list. Every time we found something genuinely good, a kitchen organizer that actually fit, a throw blanket that didn't pill after one wash, a planter that looked as good in person as it did on screen, we'd write it down and share it with friends and family. People kept asking for the list. Eventually, we figured we should just put it online.

That's what Cozy Tidy Finds is. Not a corporate review site run by people who've never opened the boxes. It's a curated collection from two real people who care about making home feel good without wasting money on things that don't deliver.

How We Pick Our Products

Every product on this site goes through the same process. We look for items with thousands of real reviews and consistently high ratings. But high ratings alone don't cut it. We read the one-star reviews first, because that's where the real problems surface: the drawer organizer that doesn't actually fit standard drawers, the towels that shed lint for six months, the "self-watering" planter that leaks all over your shelf.

Price matters, but value matters more. We'd rather recommend a $35 set of baskets that lasts three years than a $12 set that falls apart in two months. If the cheap option is genuinely good, we'll tell you. If it's not, we'll tell you that too.

The simplest test: if we wouldn't recommend it to a friend sitting across the table, it doesn't make the list. No exceptions, no matter how good the commission might be.

Privacy

We keep this simple because your privacy should be simple.

We don't collect personal data beyond what you voluntarily provide. If you sign up for our email list, your email address is stored securely through our email service provider (Kit, formerly ConvertKit). We use it to send you our weekly finds and nothing else.

We use Google Analytics to understand how people use the site: which pages get visited, how long people stick around, what devices they're using. This data is anonymous and aggregate. We can't identify you personally from it, and we don't try to.

We don't sell your information. We don't share it with third parties. We don't run retargeting ads. Your data stays between you and us.
