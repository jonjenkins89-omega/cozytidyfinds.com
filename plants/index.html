Quick Tips

Plant Parent Basics

🌱

Start Easy

Pothos, snake plants, and ZZ plants are nearly impossible to kill. Start there and build confidence.

💧

Less Water, More Often

Overwatering kills more plants than underwatering. When in doubt, wait another day.

☀

Light Matters Most

Most indoor plants want bright indirect light. A few feet from a window is usually the sweet spot.
